2. The part most shops miss

Industry default: 85%. Source: NextPhone 2026 analysis of 130,175 contractor calls, which found about 85% of callers who reach voicemail never call back. They call the next shop on the list instead. Lower it if your customers are mostly repeat clients who will try again.
