Before we start.

Will it talk to my customers without me knowing what it says?

For an install, you approve the written scope, rules and messages before anything goes live. The assistant identifies itself as an assistant. For verification, you approve the test scope before any test runs. You get the recordings and transcripts, so you can hear and read what was said.

I'm not technical. What do I have to set up?

For an install, nothing. We do the setup inside the software you already run. You approve one page of rules and the messages, then hear the test calls before anything goes live. If your agents are already live, the pilot tests what you run. Repair work is not included.

What happens if it says something wrong?

For an install, the rules are bounded: the assistant never quotes a price you did not approve or promises a slot it has not checked. Emergencies and complaints go to a human. Verification checks those boundaries against the rules you approve. The recordings and transcripts behind every test are yours to inspect.

What does the pilot include, and what is excluded?

The baseline, four weekly test cycles per location, the recordings and transcripts, and one consolidated review. The pilot covers up to 5 locations on one system. Repair work is not included. You approve the test scenarios in writing first.

How much of our time does it take?

You approve the written scope before testing. The consolidated review call is 45 minutes. Setup time varies with the systems, access and scope; we establish that with you before the pilot starts.

What systems does it work with?

We test the agents you already run. You approve which voice lines, chats and follow-ups we test before contracting. We claim no partnership or integration with any platform.

Our platform already reports on its agent.

A vendor's report is its own assessment. The pilot independently tests the surfaces your customers actually touch across the vendors and locations in your approved scope, then gives you the conversations behind the findings.

Will our failures be public?

Never. Your evidence stays private. We never publish grades.

Can I get out?

The baseline fee is earned when the baseline is delivered; if we fail to run a contracted weekly test cycle, the pilot fee is refunded pro-rata for missed cycles. Both services are month-to-month after the initial term: cancel in writing, effective at the end of the current month, no termination fee. You keep your number. Read the terms.

Who can access the recordings and transcripts?

The client keeps their copies permanently; we retain working copies for 90 days after delivery for corrections and disputes, then delete them; they are never shared, published, or used for marketing. Read the privacy policy.

Are you new? Where are your reviews?

Yes, ZeroHand is a new firm, and we publish no client results yet. That is why the deliverable, the process, and the price are all on this page: judge what you can inspect. Every engagement starts with a written scope you approve before anything runs.

What if we have no AI agents yet?

Start with the install step: missed-call text-back and bounded estimate follow-up. Verification starts once agents are live. See the install process.
