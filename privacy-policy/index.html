Privacy Policy — ZeroHand Labs

Effective date: October 15, 2026

ZeroHand Labs is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of this site. This policy describes how the app handles data when you authorize it with your Google account.

Data we access

Through the YouTube Data API (scope youtube.force-ssl), the app can:

  • Read comments on your YouTube channel and post replies to them
  • Read your channel's video metadata and manage video uploads

How we use it

Access is used solely to automate comment replies and video management on your own YouTube channel. Nothing else.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to any third party, ever.

Data storage and revocation

OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only for the purposes above. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
