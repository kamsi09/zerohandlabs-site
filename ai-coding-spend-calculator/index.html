What is your AI coding tool actually costing you?

Pick one tool, enter your seats and what you really pay, and see the monthly bill plus where this billing period lands if the current pace holds. One tool at a time, on purpose: per-tool numbers are the ones that hide.

1. Your plan

Anything billed on top of the seat price: premium request or AI credit overages on Copilot, usage-based charges on Cursor, API tokens for Claude Code or Codex. If your bill is seat price only, leave this at 0. If you only know the team total, divide it by seats.

2. The pace check

Use your billing period, not the calendar month, if they differ. Leave spend at 0 if you have not checked yet; the plan math above still works.

The pace formula: projected period spend = spend so far / days elapsed x days in the period. It is the same math behind the ZeroHand Verdict. A tool that looks fine on day 5 can be a very different number by day 30.
